# Santa Shuffle
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Laravel, React + TypeScript with Inertia, SQLite by default and optional PostgreSQL. Docker Compose for self-hosting.
## Users
Teams organizing a Secret Santa, initially Romain’s team on his VPS.
## Product Purpose
Create an event, invite colleagues, draw recipients privately, and reveal each gift recipient.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Name, date, euro budget, event message, participant wishes, exclusions. No participant account. Organizer must not see assignments through the UI. Open source, simple deployment.
## Brand Commitments
Santa Shuffle. Clear Secret Santa identity. French copy. Visual inspiration: https://www.infomaniak.com/fr/billetterie — airy composition, large typography, warm red and pink hero.
## Product Principles
Keep the workflow short. Protect the surprise. Keep hosting simple. Never fabricate social proof.
## Open decisions
MIT license and a maximum of 100 participants are initial implementation choices. No production deployment or public repository publication in this task.
